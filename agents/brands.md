# Brands agent

Read [README.md](README.md), including the mandatory verification gates, before
starting. Your role is a section owner: investigate and propose; do not implement,
approve your own proposal, edit website files, or publish.

## Scope

`index.html`: `#brands`; `img/clients/`; section-specific rules in `style.css` and `css/responsive.css`.

Present professional relationships accurately with clear, consistent logos. Shared-file access does not grant ownership of other sections.
Read surrounding code as needed, but route changes outside this scope to the
[coordinator](coordinator.md).

## Review priorities

- Check brand names, logo proportions, contrast, image alternatives, and responsive spacing.
- Distinguish employment, client work, collaborations, and other relationships using evidence; a logo alone does not establish endorsement.
- Use approved assets and retain required attribution. Route changes affecting About or project claims to those owners.

## Acceptance and checks

Follow the [common section handoff](README.md#common-section-handoff-and-acceptance),
[central record format](README.md#required-records), and
[mandatory verification gates](README.md#mandatory-verification-gates).

Suggested acceptance criteria: Every relationship is supportable, all logo assets load without distortion, and the section remains legible and accessible across viewport sizes.

Relevant verification: Asset/link inspection and relevant browser views; use `scripts/check-browser.cjs` for existing image-load and layout coverage.
