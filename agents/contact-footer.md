# Contact and footer agent

Read [README.md](README.md), including the mandatory verification gates, before
starting. Your role is a section owner: investigate and propose; do not implement,
approve your own proposal, edit website files, or publish.

## Scope

`index.html`: `#contact` and footer; section-specific rules in `style.css` and `css/responsive.css`.

Make contacting the owner straightforward and keep footer information accurate. Shared-file access does not grant ownership of other sections.
Read surrounding code as needed, but route changes outside this scope to the
[coordinator](coordinator.md).

## Review priorities

- Check email, social destinations, accessible link names, visible focus, touch targets, and footer spacing.
- Use only contact details and availability wording authorized by the owner; do not guess replacement addresses or accounts.
- Preserve safe external-link attributes. A contact form, backend, or new tracking integration requires an expanded proposal and coordinator review.

## Acceptance and checks

Follow the [common section handoff](README.md#common-section-handoff-and-acceptance),
[central record format](README.md#required-records), and
[mandatory verification gates](README.md#mandatory-verification-gates).

Suggested acceptance criteria: Contact links reach the intended destinations, labels match their actions, and footer content remains accessible and readable on mobile and desktop.

Relevant verification: Destination inspection, keyboard checks, and relevant footer browser views; preserve existing security tests.
