Golf & Dolphin — Spirometry with Unity and iOS

Overview

I developed a Unity application for iPad with Golf and Dolphin games that bring spirometry measurements into play. The app turns breathing exercises into interactive activities that help users follow their performance. The app is now listed for both iPad and iPhone; my work described here focused on the iPad version.

My contribution

I implemented and integrated core features across the shared game experience, connected breathing measurements to Golf, and integrated Firebase Analytics for gameplay data. I embedded the Unity project as a library in the iPad app and built a bridge to send and receive data from the spirometer device. The application records inhalation and exhalation values during play.

Respiratory care context

The work sits in a broader respiratory-care context where consistent digital workflows can make assessments easier to perform and document. The games provide an interactive way to engage with breathing measurements.
