About me

I’m a software engineer and technical lead with eight years of experience building real-time simulations, spatial computing systems, and interactive applications across mobile, web, and immersive platforms. I combine hands-on development in C#, Python, and Unity with modular architecture, performance optimization, and reliable build and release workflows.

At Fraunhofer ISE, I currently focus on DevOps and backend development. I previously developed the now-released Heat Pump PlanAR, an iPad-based spatial planning application for heat-pump installations that combines ARKit, LiDAR-aware workflows, persistent mapping, and optimized pipe routing. Previously, I led a 10-person multidisciplinary team and delivered three enterprise simulation programs for GSK, Abbott, and Roche. My work also spans medical training, digital twins, multiplayer systems, and AR/VR experiences—from early prototypes through production delivery.
